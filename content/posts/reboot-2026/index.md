If you look at the very first "Hello World" post on this website, written way back in January 2024, you will find a bright warning right at the top: 

{{< alert >}}
**WARN!** This post may contain unrealistic promises.
{{< /alert >}}

Well, it turns out that was a very accurate warning. Back then, after spending hours fighting with Nix and Hugo configurations, I promised to post about technology, random thoughts, and even cheesecake recipes. Fast forward to today, and that remained the only post on this page. 

So, what changed? Why am I back here writing this? 

## A new revamp!

Recently, I printed some physical business cards to hand out at conferences, labs, and academic meetings. On the back of those cards, I placed a QR Code to this's website main page. It suddenly hit me: if I am handing this QR Code to professors, researchers, and colleagues, it probably shouldn't lead to an abandoned "Hello World" page promising a cheesecake recipe that never arrived! 

This realization prompted a complete revamp. I am renewing my vows with this website, but with a shift in focus. I am officially transitioning this space from a generic blog into my personal page and professional portfolio. 

Since 2024, a lot has happened. I finished my Computer Engineering degree, started my Master's at USP (EESC), joined the Center for Robotics (CRob), and even spent time as a visiting researcher in Japan. My focus has sharpened around Aerial Robotics (UAVs), State Estimation, and Embedded Systems. From now on, this page will serve as a showcase for my academic work and research interests. 

If you look around, you will notice some changes:
* A brand new **[About Me](/personal_blog/about_me/)** section detailing my academic journey.
* A **[Projects](/personal_blog/projects/)** tab (currently under construction) where I will document my ongoing research, collaborations and other intersting things.

## Welcome

If you are reading this because you scanned the QR code on my card: **Welcome!** It is great to connect with you. Feel free to explore the new sections, check out my Lattes CV, or reach out to me via email or LinkedIn.

I won't promise weekly blog posts anymore (lesson learned!), but I do promise to keep this space updated with my latest projects and academic publications. 

(And who knows, maybe that red berry cheesecake recipe will still find its way here someday. It is still my personal page, after all!)
