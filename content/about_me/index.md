I am an academic researcher working at the intersection of **Theoretical Robotics** and **Computer Systems**, with a primary focus on aerial robotics and Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs). 

Currently, my work centers on **State Estimation**, **Numerical Optimization**, and **Embedded Systems**. I am highly interested in the co-design of algorithms and software to ensure computational efficiency and real-world applicability in autonomous systems.

You can find my complete academic history on my [Lattes Curriculum](http://lattes.cnpq.br/4263198323019177).
